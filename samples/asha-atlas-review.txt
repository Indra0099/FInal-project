I bought this laptop for programming classes and Java projects. The keyboard is comfortable and the chassis feels sturdy. In my personal use the battery was satisfactory for lectures, although I did not measure runtime scientifically. The fan becomes noticeable while compiling large projects. I would recommend it to students who value build quality and portability, while reminding them that sustained heavy workloads are audible.
